Course on research integrity and the ethical use of artificial intelligence in 
doctoral research, organized by UAH. Held on October 20 and 27, 2025.

## Contents
- Ethical foundations of AI applied to academic and doctoral contexts
- Integration of AI in the research cycle (literature review, data analysis, writing)
- Identification of risks: bias, lack of transparency, plagiarism, data protection
- Future scenarios of AI use in research and its impact on the scientific community

## Duration
4 h

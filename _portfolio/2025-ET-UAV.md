Hands-on workshop on evapotranspiration estimation using two-source (TSEB) and 
three-source (3SEB) energy balance models with high-resolution UAV imagery. 
Held on October 27, 2025.

## Contents
- Theoretical foundations of TSEB and 3SEB energy balance models
- Preprocessing of optical and thermal UAV images
- Characterization of vegetation biophysical variables (temperature, albedo, cover)
- Modelling of radiation transmission through the canopy
- Running pyTSEB and py3SEB using Jupyter Notebooks
- Evaluation of modelled results against field observations

## Duration
4 h

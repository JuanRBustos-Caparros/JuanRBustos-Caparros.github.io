Course on spectroradiometry applied to remote sensing research, organized by the 
Academic Committee of the Geographic Information Technologies programme (UAH). 
Held on November 26, 2025.

## Contents
- Fundamentals of spectroradiometry and basic instrument principles
- Field spectroradiometry: applications and measurement protocols
- Operation of ASD FieldSpec 4 spectroradiometer and accessories
- Laboratory measurements of different surfaces: clay, fresh and dry vegetation, organic matter, sand, plant canopy
- Analysis of water content effects, spectral mixtures and structural factors
- Basic spectral data processing with ViewSpecPro
- Graphical representation of reflectance curves using Python or R
- Simulation of Landsat 9 and Sentinel-2 spectral resolution effects
- Calculation of vegetation indices with hyperspectral and multispectral data

## Duration
6 h

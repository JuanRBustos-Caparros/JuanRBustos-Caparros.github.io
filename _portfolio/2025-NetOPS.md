Training course on remote and proximal sensing using spectroradiometers and drones 
for terrestrial and aquatic applications. Held from May 19 to 23, 2025.

## Contents
- Fundamentals of proximity optical remote sensing and spectroradiometry
- Field campaign planning for terrestrial and aquatic applications
- Reflectance measurements in water, soils and vegetation
- Use of drones and airborne sensors for data collection
- Vicarious calibration of sensors
- Processing and interpretation of spectral data

## Duration
40.5 h

## About me
**Born and raised in the Mediterranean**

I am a PhD student at **Environmental Remote Sensing and Spectroscropy Laboratory (SpecLab)** with a predoctoral contract from the **MOMENTUM-CSIC** program. My research focuses on the multiscale integration of remote sensing, proximal, and in-situ data to feed AI algorithms in the context of environmental applications. I obtained my Bachelor's degree in Geography from the University of Balearic Islands and a Master's degree in Natural Hazards from the University of León. During my academic training, I completed a two-month research internship at the Q-GEO group at the University of León. So far, my work has focused on hydrology and sediment transport in Mediterranean catchments.
